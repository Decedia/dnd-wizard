================================================================================
LEVEL UP WIZARD GAP ANALYSIS - Complete Report
================================================================================

Methodology: Analyzed all classes (levels 1-20) and subclasses from
en/2014_classes.json and en/2014_subclasses.json. Compared against
LevelUpWizard.tsx implementation to identify choice features that are
NOT handled by the wizard.

A choice feature is considered HANDLED if:
  1. It has 'choices' in the class/subclass data (LevelUpWizard reads it directly)
  2. It has choices in subclass_feature_choices.json (merged by getStaticSubclasses)
  3. It's in the hardcoded handled list (Pact Boon, Invocations, etc.)
